Text 01

Everyday English: McDonald’s

can · would like · his/her/my
Read the dialogue

Cashier: Hi! Welcome to McDonald’s. How can I help you?

Paulo: Hi. Can I get one Big Mac meal, please?

Cashier: Sure. Is your drink Coke or Sprite?

Paulo: Coke, please. And can I have medium fries?

Cashier: Of course. Is this order for here or to go?

Paulo: To go, please. My wife is waiting in the car.

Cashier: No problem. Your total is twelve dollars.

Paulo: Here is my card. Thank you.

1. What does Paulo order?

2. Is the order for here or to go?

3. Who is waiting in the car?

4. Complete: Paulo says, “Here is ___ card.”

Use a possessive adjective.

5. Replace “Paulo” with the correct pronoun: ___ wants Coke.

6. Complete: Paulo’s wife is waiting in the car. She is ___ wife.

7. What does “How can I help you?” mean in this situation?

Text 02

Wedding Photographer Meeting

wedding · bride · groom · timeline
Read the dialogue

Bride: Hi, Paulo. Our wedding is next week, and we want to confirm the details.

Paulo: Perfect. What time does the ceremony start?

Groom: The ceremony starts at 4 PM, but we are at the venue at 2 PM.

Paulo: Great. I can arrive at 1:30 PM to check the light and prepare my camera.

Bride: That is good. I want natural photos with my family before the ceremony.

Paulo: Of course. Do you prefer candid photos or traditional poses?

Groom: We like candid photos, but our parents want some traditional pictures too.

Paulo: No problem. I can organize both. Please send me your timeline today.

Bride: Sure. I can send it after lunch. Thank you, Paulo.

8. When is the wedding?

9. What time does the ceremony start?

10. Why does Paulo arrive at 1:30 PM?

11. What kind of photos does the couple like?

12. Replace “the bride” with the correct pronoun: ___ wants natural photos.

13. Replace “the bride and the groom” with the correct pronoun: ___ like candid photos.

14. Complete: Paulo needs to prepare ___ camera.

15. Complete: The couple says, “___ parents want traditional pictures.”

The parents belong to the couple.
